Inside a cell, and how it divides

The prompt

Cell division: examine mitosis and meiosis. Explain the whole process in a more visual way, with realistic pictures. Start with a very detailed drawing of a cell, then move on to division.

The answer in the chat

A cell is a nucleus surrounded by a dozen working parts. Mitosis makes two identical copies of a cell; meiosis divides twice and makes four eggs or sperm, each with half the chromosomes and a new mix.
