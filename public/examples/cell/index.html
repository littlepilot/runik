Cell division: mitosis and meiosis

The prompt

Cell division: examine mitosis and meiosis. Explain the whole process in a more visual way, with realistic pictures.

The answer in the chat

Mitosis makes two identical copies of a cell; meiosis divides twice and makes four eggs or sperm, each with half the chromosomes and a new mix.
