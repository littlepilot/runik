Eight espresso drinks compared

The prompt

Compare eight espresso drinks: espresso, macchiato, cortado, flat white, cappuccino, latte, americano and mocha. For each one show what is in the cup and in what proportions, how much caffeine it has, and how the milk is textured. Base it on café standards and published sources, and point out where the definitions disagree. Give it to me as a PDF.

The answer in the chat

Most of them are the same double espresso; the amount of milk, foam or water on top is what changes the drink.
