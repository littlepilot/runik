How noise-cancelling headphones work

The prompt

How do noise-cancelling headphones work? Show what is inside one, how the cancelling works step by step, the difference between feedforward, feedback and hybrid designs, which sounds it removes and why, and a short history. Research it, then build the document.

The answer in the chat

Microphones hear the noise, a chip plays the same wave upside down, and the two cancel in your ear; it works best on low, steady sounds.
