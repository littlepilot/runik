Coffee: eight drinks, their history and their beans

The prompt

Coffee. First, a side-by-side visual comparison of espresso, macchiato, cortado, flat white, cappuccino, latte, americano and mocha, showing what is in each one and the shape and size of its cup. Then a chronological story, starting with espresso: when each of the eight was first made. Then where coffee beans are grown, on a world map: what each origin is like, and which drink it is used in. Research all of this and more, then build the document.

The answer in the chat

Espresso came first in 1906 and the flat white last in the 1980s; Brazil grows over a third of the beans.
