How a lithium-ion battery works

The prompt

How does a lithium-ion battery work? Show what is inside a real cell, what happens during charging and discharging down to the atoms, how the main battery chemistries compare, why batteries wear out and how to make one last, and where the technology came from. Research it, then build the document.

The answer in the chat

Lithium ions shuttle between graphite and a metal oxide through the electrolyte, while electrons take the long way through your device.
